Konstantinos Kontras

Multimodal Machine Learning

PostDoc Researcher KU Leuven

  • CV

Hi, I'm Konstantinos

I am a PostDoc at the Multisensory Intelligence in MIT Media Lab and in ESAT at KU Leuven, working with Paul Liang and Maarten De Vos. My research studies multimodal learning and the training dynamics of models that combine heterogeneous sources of information.

I received my PhD from KU Leuven, where I worked on Multimodal Fusion and Multimodal Competition, fundamental challenges in training multimodal systems, across modalities ranging from video, text, and audio to physiological signals such as EEG, supervised by Maarten De Vos, Christos Chatzichristos, and Johan Suykens.

Before the PhD, I worked with the PSI/Visics and TRACE groups on uncertainty estimation for autonomous driving, including Bayesian model averaging and active learning for semantic segmentation, supervised by Mark Proesmans and Luc Van Gool.
